`Unreal, Blueprints, GitHub`

[GitHub](https://github.com/ro-kauffman/alligator_gravity_minigame)

---

## Overview
Pharaoh's Forces is a 3v1 mini-game made to help kids learn how to use their prosthetics. In this asymmetrical battle, the team of 3 tries to reach the bottom of a waterfall flowing in reverse, while the solo player throws blocks to thwart them.

This project was made for the Limbitless x UCF x UU game jam, an event where teams had 48 hours to make a game for Limbitless Solutions Bionic Bash. At this point the Limbitless team had a template for 3v1 games but had not yet made one, our team wanted to make the first. I led development for the solo player, implementing the mechanic to choose a lane and throw rocks.

This was my first ever project for Unreal
