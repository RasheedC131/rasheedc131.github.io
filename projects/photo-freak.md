# Photo Freak

`Unity, C#, GitHub`

[GitHub](https://github.com/RasheedC131/PhotoFreak)

---

## Overview
Photo Freak is a photography horror game made to apply the design patterns, algorithms, and data structures learned in class. In it you must snap photos of monsters disguised at a gala while trying to fit in so you don't get kicked out.

This project was made for UCF's AI for Game Programming course. We had a semester to make a game, meeting typical production deadlines along the way. For this project I developed the photo system, taking massive inspiration from Pokémon snap for the scoring logic.
