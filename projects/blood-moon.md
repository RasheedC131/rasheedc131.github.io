## Overview
Blood Moon is a resource-management turn-based RPG made to get experience in development you won't get in class. In it you must manage a cult, using your followers' health to cast spells. Success requires balancing squad preservation with offensive output.

This project was made for Knight Hacks VIII, a 36-hour hackathon. While this was only my second hackathon and third game project ever, it marked my first time stepping into a technical leadership and project management role.  I took ownership of the global systems architecture and asset integration, while helping teammates with the basics of the Godot engine.
